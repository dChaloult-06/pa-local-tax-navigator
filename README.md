## What This Tool Covers

Pennsylvania has one of the most complex local tax systems in the United States — 2,500+ jurisdictions, 69 Tax Collection Districts, multiple special-rule cities, and cross-border rules with neighboring states that most reference materials address incompletely.

| Section | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **DCED Setup Lookup** | Paste DCED address search results to generate a complete payroll setup directive: EIT rate, LST per-period withholding, collector information, and employer action steps |
| **Act 32 / EIT** | Higher-Of rule, PSD codes, Residency Certification Form, employer setup checklist |
| **Local Services Tax** | Rates, proration by pay frequency, low-income exemption, concurrent employment rules |
| **Philadelphia Wage Tax** | Current rates with effective dates (3.735% resident / 3.425% non-resident, eff. July 1, 2026), four residency scenarios, Act 48, convenience-of-employer doctrine, out-of-state employees, no LST |
| **Pittsburgh Special Rules** | EIT and LST by PSD code (700102 Pittsburgh S D / 730105 Baldwin-Whitehall S D), Payroll Expense Tax (employer-side), Allegheny County TCD structure |
| **Out-of-State Employees** | 880000 PSD code, out-of-state resident / PA worksite, PA resident / out-of-state employer, courtesy withholding |
| **PA–Maryland Deep Dive** | York/Adams 1981 legal settlement, TCD-level local exemption, the reciprocal mechanism, Form 515 trigger, MD Form 502CR |
| **Tax Collectors** | Major collector directory with employer portal links |

## Key Compliance Points

- The DCED-official **880000 out-of-state PSD code** and exactly when to use it
- **LST proration by pay frequency** with per-period withholding calculation and the $10 threshold rule
- The **York and Adams County PA–MD EIT exemption** (1981 legal settlement) — and why LST still applies even when EIT does not
- **Philadelphia and Pittsburgh** as special-rule jurisdictions — Philadelphia entirely outside Act 32; Pittsburgh's EIT under Act 32 (Allegheny Central and Southwest TCDs), with LST and Payroll Expense Tax handled by the city
- **Pittsburgh's two PSD codes** — 700102 (Pittsburgh S D, 3.0% resident EIT, $52 municipal LST) and 730105 (Baldwin-Whitehall S D, 1.5% resident EIT, $47/$5 LST split) — and lookup alerts keyed to both
- **Philadelphia levies no LST** — only the Wage Tax applies at a Philadelphia worksite
- The **NJ-COJ and MD Form 502CR** employee credit advisory layer, with clear distinction between employer withholding obligations and employee personal return actions
- **PA resident / out-of-state employer** courtesy withholding guidance, including quarterly estimated payment obligations when employers choose not to withhold
- **Multi-collector support** — the DCED Setup Lookup handles all collector configurations returned by the DCED address search, including jurisdictions where EIT, Municipal LST, and School District LST have separate collectors
